A practice for software that lasts

Code is not enough to carry a system forward.

Every meaningful change depends on a working theory: what the software is for, why it is shaped this way, which tradeoffs still matter, and what the team has learned from reality. Augmented Workflow helps leave the evidence a later contributor needs to rebuild and extend that understanding.

For teams bringing agents into real product work

Continuity breaks in ordinary moments.

The loss rarely announces itself as missing documentation. It shows up when someone must explain a choice, judge a new constraint, or make a change without knowing what the system is protecting.

Leave evidence people can use.

A repository can preserve useful traces of a team's understanding. Those traces make it easier to reconstruct, test, challenge, and renew a working theory as the software changes.

The central discipline

Keep different kinds of knowledge in forms that can be found, questioned, and updated by the people responsible for the next change.

Current intent

Living specs

State what the product should do now and make change visible when that intent moves.

Why

Decisions

Preserve the reasoning behind consequential choices, including what later evidence may supersede.

What reality taught

Learnings

Carry forward corrections and patterns that would otherwise be rediscovered in another session.

What was checked

Verification

Make the basis for confidence inspectable when behavior, assumptions, or evidence are challenged.

More participants, one standard of care.

Agents widen who can contribute to software work. They also make weak context visible quickly. The practice asks every contributor to show their basis, name uncertainty, and leave the next contributor with more than a result.

People

Bring experience, judgment, and accountability for the consequences of a change.

  • Clarify what people should understand, accomplish, and feel
  • Inspect the agent's interpretation before implementation
  • Choose among competing tradeoffs
  • Review what deserves to become durable knowledge

Agents

Can traverse the same evidence, surface gaps, and contribute work that can be inspected and reviewed.

  • Read current intent and relevant history
  • Explain how goals and references inform a proposed experience
  • Suggest a small prototype when it would clarify intent, and wait for agreement
  • Return decisions, checks, and open questions to the team
The outcome

Software a later contributor can understand again.

Augmented Workflow gives a team a repeatable way to keep intent, reasoning, correction, and verification close to the work. That makes the next change easier to justify and safer to carry through.
